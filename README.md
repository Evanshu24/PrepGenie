## Purpose of the project
I am building a platform that helps users prepare for job interviews. Users can upload their resume and select the role they want to interview for. The platform then conducts an AI-powered mock interview tailored to their resume and chosen role.
After the interview, the platform evaluates the user's performance and provides a score out of 100, along with detailed feedback on their strengths and areas for improvement.
Each user has a personalized dashboard where they can view their previous mock interviews, track their progress over time, and monitor improvements in their interview performance.
